\subsection*{A1\quad Program feature definitions}
\label{app:programs}

All sixteen program scores are literature-fixed before any model fitting and are
never re-derived from held-out data. Each score is a coverage-adjusted
within-sample rank contrast between its positive set and its negative-control
set, computed after per-accession scaling. A program is emitted only when at
least \texttt{min} of its member genes are present in the input matrix;

\subsection*{A3\quad Platform transferability diagnostic}
\label{app:transferability}

The primary analysis includes all eight eligible development studies. The
seven-study result is a post hoc sensitivity analysis: GSE173900 was excluded
only after observing its outer-fold failure. Its inclusion rule was therefore
not independent of test performance.

\paragraph{Diagnostic evidence.} The following historical grid uses held-out
labels to describe observed failures. It is not a prospective eligibility rule
and does not establish a platform-wide limitation.

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Held-out study & Configs & Median macro F1 & \textbf{Max macro F1} & \textbf{Max AUROC} & Donors \\
\midrule
E-MTAB-4945 & 56 & 0.762 & 0.899 & 0.795 & 10 \\
GSE158395   & 56 & 0.883 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 9 \\
GSE181297   & 56 & 0.829 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 6 \\
GSE181316   & 48 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 4 \\
GSE190626   & 48 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 6 \\
GSE92566    & 56 & 0.250 & 1.000 & 1.000 & 3 \\
Sun\,\&\,Burns & 56 & 0.750 & 0.913 & 0.985 & 1 \\
\midrule
\textbf{GSE173900} & 48 & \textbf{0.325} & \textbf{0.416} & \textbf{0.450} & 9 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

\paragraph{Interpretation.} Every retained cohort reaches at least 0.899 macro
F1 and 0.795 AUROC under \emph{some} configuration. \texttt{GSE173900} peaks at
0.416 macro F1 and 0.450 AUROC across all 48 of its configurations --- below
chance in AUROC terms. Fibrosis markers (POSTN, COL1A1) are directionally
\emph{inverted} on this cohort relative to every other tissue study after
harmonization. This is not a label swap: the KC (control) / KL (keloid)
assignment was verified against the source metadata. Train-only normalization
(accession $z$-score, reference quantile, ComBat-lite) and calibrated soft-vote
ensembling do not recover the fold.

\paragraph{Status.} The full eight-study historical ensemble gives mean
macro F1 0.826 and worst-fold F1 0.182, failing the existing development gates.
The seven-study mean 0.918 is exploratory. A label-dependent AUROC rule remains
outcome-dependent even if written in advance. Future eligibility rules must
use information available without held-out outcomes and be independently validated.
GSE185309 was scored in an earlier experiment and was not rescored in this
revision; it must not be described as untouched.

\subsection*{A7\quad Selective triage}
\label{app:selective}

The system may abstain when $\max(p, 1-p)$ falls below a confidence threshold.
The following historical policies were evaluated on the post hoc seven-study subset. They are secondary; the all-eight-study coverage table is in results/reviewer\_revision/selective\_by\_study.csv.

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccccc@{}}
\toprule
Policy & Mean sel.\ F1 & Worst sel.\ F1 & Mean cov. & Min cov. & Dual-class folds \\
\midrule
Inner-OOF-tuned threshold & 0.651 & 0.000 & 0.543 & 0.000 & 4/7 \\
\textbf{Fixed conf.\ $\geq0.55$} & \textbf{0.929} & 0.652 & \textbf{0.804} & 0.600 & \textbf{7/7} \\

\paragraph{Why the tuned policy is not used.} Thresholds tuned on inner
out-of-fold predictions over-abstain when transferred to outer folds: coverage
collapses to zero on \texttt{GSE181297} and to 0.20 on \texttt{GSE181316},
leaving those folds single-class and non-evaluable. The reported triage arm
therefore uses a \emph{pre-specified} fixed threshold of 0.55, with both
classes required in the kept set. At that setting the arm clears the
$\text{F1}\geq0.90$ at $\text{coverage}\geq0.60$ criterion but not the
worst-fold $\geq0.75$ criterion; the binding fold is E-MTAB-4945 at 0.652.

\subsection*{A8\quad Copilot specification and faithfulness audit}
\label{app:copilot}

\paragraph{Architecture.} The language layer is strictly downstream of
inference. The classifier produces a probability, label, confidence, abstention
decision, and per-feature linear contributions; these are serialized into an
\emph{evidence packet}; the packet is the language model's only input. The
model's output is rendered as display text and is never fed back into scoring.
The interface therefore cannot hallucinate a prediction --- only, in principle,
a description of one.

\paragraph{Evidence packet schema.}

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{8.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
Field & Contents \\
\midrule
\texttt{system}, \texttt{intended\_use}, \texttt{not\_for} & Fixed strings pinning research-use scope \\
\texttt{sample} & Accession; \texttt{sample\_id} replaced by \texttt{"redacted"} before transmission \\
\texttt{quality\_control} & Profile and gene counts, pinned-gene coverage, usable/total programs, transfer status and note \\
\texttt{prediction} & \texttt{prob\_keloid}, \texttt{pred\_label}, \texttt{decision}, \texttt{confidence}, \texttt{decision\_threshold}, \texttt{confidence\_threshold}, \texttt{feature\_set}, \texttt{model\_name}, \texttt{artifact\_stage} \\
\texttt{top\_program\_scores} & Six highest-magnitude program scores with direction \\
\texttt{top\_supporting\_} / \texttt{opposing\_contributions} & Three signed linear contributions each \\
\texttt{evidence\_scope} & Full-cohort context (0.826 mean macro F1; 101 profiles; 8 studies), with the post hoc subset explicitly labeled and an explicit warning that the live artifact and the nested LOSO ensemble are distinct evidence objects \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

\paragraph{Generation contract.} The language model writes only two or three
sentences stating which supplied programs pushed the score toward keloid or
non-keloid. It may not use digits, class statements, mechanisms, or citations.
Output is capped at 60 words, with greedy decoding and at most 160 tokens. Code
then inserts the factual opening (probability, decision, confidence), the
transfer note, and the research-use disclaimer, so no number or class label is
model-generated. Raw expression and sample identifiers are excluded from the
outgoing packet.

\paragraph{Audit.} The harness rejects an unconfigured LLM audit, records actual
request/response provenance, and retains raw outputs before runtime validation.
Automated checks cover the exact factual opening, added numbers, decision
consistency, abstention disclosure, the QC note, a final disclaimer,
clause-level contribution direction, prohibited claims, length, request
redaction, packet immutability, and truncation. These checks are screening
tools and do not establish semantic faithfulness by themselves. The model
(Qwen3-8B, pinned revision) and protocol were fixed before generation. The audit
ran on the 101 frozen evidence packets (21 abstentions), with three greedy
requests per packet; repeats are clustered by profile.

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
Arm & All checks pass & Profiles passing every repeat \\
\midrule
Deterministic template & 101/101 & 101/101 \\
LLM narrative & \textbf{290/303} (95.7\%) & 96/101 \\
\quad also English-only & 215/303 (71.0\%) & \\
\midrule
\quad keloid decisions & 128/135 & \\
\quad non-keloid decisions & 105/105 & \\
\quad abstentions & 57/63 & \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

\smallskip
All 303 generations completed without truncation. All 13 failures assigned a
program to the wrong direction. Seventy-five outputs contained a code-switched
non-English token that no check covered. The profile-cluster bootstrap 95\%
interval is 0.917--0.990. \emph{Runtime policy:} the demo displays generated
prose only when it passes every check and is English-only; otherwise it shows
the labeled template, and fallback is never counted as successful generation.
\emph{Limitations:} the narrative format and checks were developed on these same
development profiles, so this is not an independent validation. Independent
human rating was not performed; blank two-rater forms and a rubric are released.

(\texttt{qwen3\_8b\_v2/}); protocol \texttt{protocols/narrative\_local\_v2.json}.
