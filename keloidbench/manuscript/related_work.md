# Related publications for KeloidBench / SpheroScar

Every entry in sections 1–9 was resolved against NCBI E-utilities (PubMed/GEO) or
Crossref during compilation; author, journal, volume, pages, and DOI are taken from
those records rather than from recall. Section 10 lists items that could not be
machine-verified and section 11 lists datasets in the corpus that have no linked
publication.

Accession tags in brackets mark the cohort each paper contributes to the harmonized
corpus (`data/raw/public_keloid_cohorts.json`).
