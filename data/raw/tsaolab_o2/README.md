# Tsao lab oxygen-tension bulk RNA-seq (`tsaolab_o2`)

In-house bulk RNA-seq of cultured cells grown at 20% O₂, 1% O₂, or switched between the two.
Two sequencing orders from Quintara Biosciences, 60 samples in total, all in triplicate.

## Design

Every cell line went through the same four oxygen arms, three replicates each:

| Arm | `oxygen` | `treatment` (KeloidBench) |
|---|---|---|
| 20% O₂ throughout (baseline) | `20%` | `none` |
| 20% → 1% O₂ | `20%->1%` | `normoxia_to_hypoxia_1pct` |
| 1% O₂ throughout | `1%` | `hypoxia_1pct` |
| 1% → 20% O₂ | `1%->20%` | `hypoxia_1pct_to_normoxia` |

| Order | Collected | Samples | Submitted as | Actual identity (from RNA) | Species | Groups |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 1–12 | HL-1 cardiomyocytes | HL-1 | mouse | A–D |
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 13–24 | NIH3T3 fibroblasts (mouse) | **HEK293-like human cells** (see QC) | **human** | E–H |
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 25–36 | BJ fibroblasts (normal foreskin) | BJ; **10 of 12 contain HEK293-like cells** | human | I–L |
| 1030390 | 2025-05-17 | 1–12 | ATCC keloid fibroblast line | keloid fibroblasts | human | 1–4 |
| 1030390 | 2025-05-09 | 13–24 | AC16 cardiomyocytes | AC16 | human | 5–8 |

`sample_sheets/` has one row per sample, generated by `scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/make_sample_sheets.py`
from the Quintara submission forms. `submitted_as` records the label on the form, and `cell_line`/`species` record
the identity the RNA supports. Sample 17 is mis-typed as "27" in both forms; the sheets use 17.

## Cell-identity QC (`qc/`, `scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/{screen_ad5.sh,qc_identity.py}`)

- **Samples 13–24 are not NIH3T3.** About 93% of reads map to human and about 21% to mouse; the mouse HL-1 samples
  show the reverse pattern. They are female (XIST high, no chrY transcripts), and fibroblast genes are essentially off
  (COL1A1/2, COL3A1, DCN, LUM, THY1). They express LIN28B, NEFL, LHX2 and PAX2, with high CDKN2A.
  Every sample carries human adenovirus 5 transcripts, and over 99% of them fall in the E1A/E1B region
  (`qc/ad5_screen_*.tsv`: 970–2,400 E1 read pairs per million). An integrated Ad5 E1 fragment is the defining feature of HEK293.
  They are quantified against the human reference and labelled `HEK293-like`. Confirming the line needs STR profiling.
- **BJ samples carry HEK293-like admixture.** BJ is male. In 10 of the 12 BJ samples, Ad5 E1 reads (112–270 per million),
  XIST and LIN28B appear together. Relative to the HEK293-like samples, this is about 7–18% HEK293-like material.
  Q27 (0 E1 reads) and Q35 (17 per million) are clean.
  Because the level varies by sample, and two samples from the same run are clean, this points to contamination in
  culture or RNA prep rather than index hopping. Affected samples get `quality_flag=suspect` and
  `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=False`.
- **The keloid line splits by chrY, exactly along the oxygen arms.** Samples 1–6 (groups 1–2, start at 20% O₂, passage 13)
  express chrY genes (about 380–420 TPM). Samples 7–12 (groups 3–4, start at 1% O₂, passage 7) have none (≤0.3 TPM),
  and XIST is off as well. Possible causes are Y loss in culture or a different stock or donor. Either way, the
  20%-start vs 1%-start contrast within the keloid line is confounded with this difference. `qc_note` records it per sample.
- HEK293-like samples 19 and 22 carry trace chrY (7.4 TPM, about 1.4% male-cell material, likely BJ). This is noted but not flagged.
- AC16 is chrY-negative with low XIST (6–26 TPM), consistent with a female line whose XIST has eroded in culture.
- All 1030390 and HL-1 samples have 0 Ad5 reads. `qc/qc_identity.csv` has every per-sample value.

## Sequencing

Paired-end, stranded (salmon detects `ISR`). 0013831748 is split over two lanes (L001, L002), and 1030390 is on one lane (L001).
The sequencer runs, read from the FASTQ headers, are listed below and recorded per sample in `sequencing_run` and `platform_id`:

| Samples | Instrument | Run | Read length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0013831748: all 36 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:AVITI-MGH-36S-081624` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: 1–6, 10–24 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:rna0606` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: 7, 9 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:20250624_RNA_Nike` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: **8** | **Illumina NextSeq** | `NB502013:3` | 2×74 |

Keloid samples 7–9 (group 3, 1% O₂) were sequenced apart from the rest of their order, probably as a resequence.
This is a second batch effect within the 1%-start keloid arms. It does not explain the chrY split, because samples 10–12
are on the main run and are also chrY-negative.
The raw FASTQs (124 GB + 134 GB) are not in this repo. The 0013831748 FASTQs were verified against
Quintara's `md5sum.txt` and the 1030390 FASTQs against Google Drive's MD5s.

## Processing (`scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/`)

1. `fastp` adapter and quality trimming, one run per lane.
2. `salmon quant` 1.10.3 (`-l A --validateMappings --gcBias --seqBias`), with both lanes pooled per sample, against
   GENCODE human v46 or mouse vM35 transcriptomes (transcriptome-only index, k=31).
3. Transcripts are summed to genes, using Ensembl gene IDs without version.
4. `build_keloidbench.py` writes the KeloidBench/DermTrace inputs, one set per species:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_tpm_symbol.tsv.gz` | gene symbol × sample, TPM, **not logged** (KeloidBench applies `log1p` itself) |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_counts_symbol.tsv.gz` | gene symbol × sample, salmon estimated counts |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_tpm_ensembl.tsv.gz`, `_counts_ensembl.tsv.gz` | same, keyed by Ensembl gene ID |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_sample_metadata.csv` | KeloidBench `METADATA_COLUMNS` plus `species, submitted_as, cell_line, group, oxygen, collection_date, order_id, num_fragments, mapping_rate_pct, quality_flag, qc_notes, xist_tpm, chrY_tpm, ad5_e1_per_million, hek_admixture_est_pct, sex_inferred, sequencing_run` (`platform_id` is the instrument) |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_genes.tsv.gz` | Ensembl gene ID → gene symbol and gene type |

Further outputs:

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `per_order/<order>_<species>_harmonizable-USE.tsv.gz` | Ensembl gene ID × sample, **log2(TPM+1)**, one table per order and species. Same layout as the lab's Korean keloid `-USE` files, for use outside KeloidBench |
| `per_order/<order>_<species>_counts.tsv.gz`, `_genes.tsv.gz`, `.meta-USE.csv` | Matching counts, gene annotation and basic sample metadata (no identity-QC columns; see `qc/`) |
| `salmon_quant/<order>/<sample>/quant.sf.gz` | Transcript-level salmon output (gzipped `quant.sf`), with `cmd_info.json`, `lib_format_counts.json` and `aux_info/meta_info.json`. Readable by tximport. Local paths are replaced with `<LAB>` |
| `qc/run_qc.tsv` | Per sample: instrument, run, raw and trimmed reads, % adapter-trimmed, % Q30, duplication, mapping rate, library type, fragment length (`run_qc_summary.py`) |
| `qc/qc_identity.csv`, `qc/ad5_screen_*.tsv` | Cell-identity QC (see above) |

Within a sample, TPM values from duplicated gene symbols are summed. Genes with no reads in any sample are dropped.
Human and mouse are kept separate because no ortholog mapping has been applied.

| Species | Samples | Gene symbols | Mapping rate | `quality_flag` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| human | 48: keloid 12, BJ 12, AC16 12, HEK293-like 12 | 43,478 | 86–97% | 38 ok, 10 suspect (BJ admixture) |
| mouse | 12: HL-1 | 34,195 | 92–94% | 12 ok |

Only 14 samples have `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=True`: the 12 keloid samples and the clean BJ samples Q27 and Q35.

## Labelling and caveats

- `sample_id` is the Quintara ID, e.g. `1030390-7`. `accession` is `TSAO_QB<order>`. `patient_id` is the cell line.
- Only human skin fibroblasts get a keloid label: the ATCC keloid line is `keloid` and BJ is `normal`. HEK293-like samples get no disease label.
  Cardiomyocytes, HEK293-like and mouse samples are `unknown`; they and QC-suspect samples have `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=False`.
- **All 12 keloid samples come from a single cell line, so there is one donor.** Replicates are not independent donors.
- **Keloid vs normal is confounded with sequencing order.** BJ was sequenced in 2024 and the keloid line in 2025, and only BJ Q27 and Q35 are clean.
  BJ is also neonatal foreskin, not adult skin.
- Each `accession` mixes fibroblasts and cardiomyocytes. A per-accession z-score would therefore be dominated by cell type,
  so consider giving each cell line its own accession before harmonising.
- `cohort_role` is `unassigned`. Decide its development or lockbox role before adding it to `public_keloid_cohorts.json`.
