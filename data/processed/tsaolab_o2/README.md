# Tsao lab oxygen-tension bulk RNA-seq (`tsaolab_o2`)

In-house bulk RNA-seq of cultured cells grown at 20% O₂, 1% O₂, or switched between the two.
Two sequencing orders from Quintara Biosciences, 60 samples in total, all in triplicate.

## Design

Every submitted line went through the same four oxygen arms, three replicates each:

| Arm | `oxygen` | `treatment` (DermTrace) |
|---|---|---|
| 20% O₂ throughout (baseline) | `20%` | `none` |
| 20% → 1% O₂ | `20%->1%` | `normoxia_to_hypoxia_1pct` |
| 1% O₂ throughout | `1%` | `hypoxia_1pct` |
| 1% → 20% O₂ | `1%->20%` | `hypoxia_1pct_to_normoxia` |

Three groups of samples are not what the submission form says (see QC):

| Order | Collected | Samples | Submitted as | Actual identity (from RNA) | Species | Groups |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 1–12 | HL-1 cardiomyocytes | HL-1 | mouse | A–D |
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 13–24 (all 12 NIH3T3 tubes) | NIH3T3 fibroblasts (mouse) | **HEK293-like human cells**; 19 and 22 also carry ~10% mouse cells | **human** | E–H |
| 0013831748 | 2024-07-30 | 25–36 | BJ fibroblasts (normal foreskin) | BJ; **10 of 12 contain HEK293-like cells** | human | I–L |
| 1030390 | 2025-05-17 | 1–6 | ATCC keloid fibroblast line | **BJ** (SNP genotype) | human | 1–2 |
| 1030390 | 2025-05-17 | 7–12 | ATCC keloid fibroblast line | keloid fibroblasts, ATCC CRL-1762 (KEL FIB) | human | 3–4 |
| 1030390 | 2025-05-09 | 13–24 | AC16 cardiomyocytes | AC16 | human | 5–8 |

So the keloid line was only grown in the two arms that start at 1% O₂, and the 1030390 "keloid" tubes of the two 20%-start arms
hold BJ.

`sample_sheets/` has one row per sample, generated by `scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/make_sample_sheets.py`
from the Quintara submission forms. `submitted_as` records the label on the form, and `cell_line`/`species` record
the identity the RNA supports. Sample 17 is mis-typed as "27" in both forms; the sheets use 17.

## Cell-identity QC (`qc/`, `scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/{screen_ad5.sh,qc_identity.py,genotype_check.sh,genotype.py}`)

- **Samples 13–24 are not NIH3T3.** Quantified against the human transcriptome, 92–94% of their reads map; against the
  mouse transcriptome (a separate run, from when they were still labelled mouse) only about 20–22% do, which is the rate
  at which human reads cross-map. HL-1 shows the reverse (92–94% to mouse). They are female (XIST high, no RPS4Y1),
  and fibroblast genes are essentially off (COL1A1/2, COL3A1, DCN, LUM, THY1). They express LIN28B, NEFL, LHX2 and
  PAX2, with high CDKN2A. Every sample carries human adenovirus 5 transcripts, and over 99% of them fall in the E1A/E1B
  region (`qc/ad5_screen_*.tsv`: 970–2,400 E1 read pairs per million). An integrated Ad5 E1 fragment is the defining
  feature of HEK293. They are quantified against the human reference and labelled `HEK293-like`. Confirming the line
  needs STR profiling.
- **Samples 19 and 22 also contain mouse cells, about 10% of reads.** They map 27% to the mouse transcriptome instead of
  20%, and 86% to the human one instead of 93%. In the mouse-index runs, mouse fibroblast transcripts (Col1a2, Sparc,
  S100a4, Thy1) are 30–100 times those of samples 13–18, so these are probably real NIH3T3 cells that the HEK293-like
  cells overgrew. Their small chrY signal (7.4 TPM, DDX3Y and EIF1AY with no RPS4Y1) is mouse Y transcripts mapped
  to the human genes, not male human cells. Both are `quality_flag=suspect` (`mouse_admixture_est_pct`).
  Samples 20, 21, 23 and 24 carry about 1–2%: their mouse fibroblast transcripts are 5–10 times those of 13–18.
  The mapping-rate estimate is only good to about ±1%, so values near 1% elsewhere are noise.
- **BJ samples carry HEK293-like admixture.** BJ is male. In 10 of the 12 BJ samples of 0013831748, Ad5 E1 reads
  (112–270 per million), XIST and LIN28B appear together. Relative to the HEK293-like samples, this is about 7–18%
  HEK293-like material; fitting each sample as a mix of the clean BJ and the HEK293-like profiles gives a similar 7–23%.
  Q27 (0 E1 reads) and Q35 (17 per million) are clean. Q27 is in the 20% arm and Q35 in the 1%→20% arm, so the two
  hypoxic BJ arms of this order have no clean sample.
  Because the level varies by sample, and two samples from the same run are clean, this points to contamination in
  culture or RNA prep rather than index hopping. Affected samples get `quality_flag=suspect` and
  `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=False`.
- **1030390 samples 1–6 are BJ, not the keloid line.** SNP genotypes called from 2M read pairs per sample
  (`genotype_check.sh`, `genotype.py`) group them with the BJ samples of 0013831748. They disagree with the clean BJ
  samples Q27 and Q35 at 0.2–1.1% of sites, the same as Q27 and Q35 disagree with each other (1.0%). Samples 7–12
  instead match public RNA-seq of ATCC CRL-1762 (KEL FIB, GSE232079 run SRR24485856), at 5–8% (another lab and
  sequencer). The two halves disagree at 72–77%, as unrelated donors do (`qc/genotype_match.tsv`,
  `qc/genotype_discordance.tsv`). Expression agrees: samples 1–6 are male
  (chrY about 380–420 TPM), GSTM1-null and HOXA13+, like BJ (neonatal foreskin). Samples 7–12 are chrY-negative,
  GSTM1+ and HOX-negative, and CRL-1762 is female (Cellosaurus CVCL_3730), with XIST eroded in culture.
  Samples 1–6 are relabelled `cell_line=BJ` (`submitted_as=ATCC_keloid`). They carry no Ad5 reads, so they are clean BJ.
  An STR profile compared with ATCC's CRL-1762 profile would confirm this independently.
- AC16 is chrY-negative with low XIST (6–26 TPM), consistent with a female line whose XIST has eroded in culture.
- All 1030390 and HL-1 samples have 0 Ad5 reads. `qc/qc_identity.csv` has every per-sample value, and the
  per-sample notes are in `qc_note` there and `qc_notes` in the sample metadata.

The genotype check needs minimap2, samtools and bcftools, which are not in the `rnaseq` env
(`conda create -p <env> -c conda-forge -c bioconda minimap2 samtools bcftools`, then `GENO_ENV=<env>`).
Sites with an ambiguous allele fraction are left uncalled, so replicates of one culture disagree at 0–2% of sites and
unrelated donors at 60–80%. Admixture raises the figure a little: the contaminated BJ samples sit at 1–9% against
clean BJ, and HEK293-like 19 and 22 (mouse reads) at up to 17% against the other HEK293-like samples. All 48 human
samples fall into four donors that match `cell_line`: BJ (18), the keloid line with the CRL-1762 reference (6 + 1),
HEK293-like (12) and AC16 (12).

## Sequencing

Paired-end, stranded (salmon detects `ISR`). 0013831748 is split over two lanes (L001, L002), and 1030390 is on one lane (L001).
The sequencer runs, read from the FASTQ headers, are listed below and recorded per sample in `sequencing_run` and `platform_id`:

| Samples | Instrument | Run | Read length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0013831748: all 36 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:AVITI-MGH-36S-081624` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: 1–6, 10–24 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:rna0606` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: 7, 9 | Element AVITI | `AV234402:20250624_RNA_Nike` | 2×76 |
| 1030390: **8** | **Illumina NextSeq** | `NB502013:3` | 2×73–76 (already trimmed) |

Keloid samples 7–9 (group 3, 1% O₂) were sequenced apart from the rest of their order, probably as a resequence.
This is a batch effect between the two keloid arms: group 3 (1% throughout) is on two other runs, while group 4
(1%→20%) is on the main run.
The raw FASTQs (124 GB + 134 GB) are not in this repo. The 0013831748 FASTQs were verified against
Quintara's `md5sum.txt` and the 1030390 FASTQs against Google Drive's MD5s.

## Processing (`scripts/tsaolab_rnaseq/`)

1. `fastp` adapter and quality trimming, one run per lane.
2. `salmon quant` 1.10.3 (`-l A --validateMappings --gcBias --seqBias`), with both lanes pooled per sample, against
   GENCODE human v46 or mouse vM35 transcriptomes (transcriptome-only index, k=31).
3. Transcripts are summed to genes, using Ensembl gene IDs without version.
4. `build_dermtrace.py` writes the DermTrace inputs, one set per species:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_tpm_symbol.tsv.gz` | gene symbol × sample, TPM, **not logged** (the DermTrace loaders apply `log1p` themselves) |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_counts_symbol.tsv.gz` | gene symbol × sample, salmon estimated counts |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_tpm_ensembl.tsv.gz`, `_counts_ensembl.tsv.gz` | same, keyed by Ensembl gene ID |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_sample_metadata.csv` | `METADATA_COLUMNS` (`keloidbench/scripts/expression_processing.py`) plus `species, submitted_as, cell_line, group, oxygen, collection_date, order_id, num_fragments, mapping_rate_pct, quality_flag, qc_notes, xist_tpm, chrY_tpm, ad5_e1_per_million, hek_admixture_est_pct, mouse_admixture_est_pct, sex_inferred, sequencing_run` (`platform_id` is the instrument) |
| `tsaolab_o2_<species>_genes.tsv.gz` | Ensembl gene ID → gene symbol and gene type |

Further outputs:

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `per_order/<order>_<species>_harmonizable-USE.tsv.gz` | Ensembl gene ID × sample, **log2(TPM+1)**, one table per order and species. Same layout as the lab's Korean keloid `-USE` files, for use outside DermTrace |
| `per_order/<order>_<species>_counts.tsv.gz`, `_genes.tsv.gz`, `.meta-USE.csv` | Matching counts, gene annotation and basic sample metadata (no identity-QC columns; see `qc/`) |
| `salmon_quant/<order>/<sample>/quant.sf.gz` | Transcript-level salmon output (gzipped `quant.sf`), with `cmd_info.json`, `lib_format_counts.json` and `aux_info/meta_info.json`. Readable by tximport. Local paths are replaced with `<LAB>` |
| `qc/run_qc.tsv` | Per sample: instrument, run, raw and trimmed reads, % adapter-trimmed, % Q30, duplication, mapping rate, library type, fragment length (`run_qc_summary.py`) |
| `qc/qc_identity.csv`, `qc/ad5_screen_*.tsv` | Cell-identity QC (see above) |
| `qc/genotype_match.tsv`, `qc/genotype_discordance.tsv` | Donor group of every human sample and the CRL-1762 reference, and pairwise % genotype discordance |

Within a sample, TPM values from duplicated gene symbols are summed. Genes with no reads in any sample are dropped.
Human and mouse are kept separate because no ortholog mapping has been applied.

| Species | Samples | Gene symbols | Mapping rate | `quality_flag` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| human | 48: keloid 6, BJ 18, AC16 12, HEK293-like 12 | 43,478 | 86–97% | 36 ok; 12 suspect (10 BJ with HEK293-like admixture, 2 HEK293-like with mouse cells) |
| mouse | 12: HL-1 | 34,195 | 92–94% | 12 ok |

Only 14 samples have `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=True`: the 6 keloid samples (1030390-7 to -12) and the 8 clean
BJ samples (1030390-1 to -6, Q27 and Q35).

## Labelling and caveats

- `sample_id` is the Quintara ID, e.g. `1030390-7`. `accession` is `TSAO_QB<order>`. `patient_id` is the cell line,
  so the BJ samples of both orders share `patient_id=BJ`.
- Only human skin fibroblasts get a keloid label: the ATCC keloid line is `keloid` and BJ is `normal`. HEK293-like samples get no disease label.
  Cardiomyocytes, HEK293-like and mouse samples are `unknown`; they and QC-suspect samples have `eligible_for_keloid_pretraining=False`.
- **All 6 keloid samples come from a single cell line (ATCC CRL-1762), so there is one donor.** Replicates are not independent donors.
- **Keloid vs normal is confounded with oxygen arm and with sequencing order.** In 1030390 the keloid line is only in
  the 1%-start arms and BJ only in the 20%-start arms. The only arm with both clean keloid and clean BJ is 1%→20%
  (keloid 10–12 against BJ Q35, which is from the 2024 order). BJ is also neonatal foreskin, not adult skin.
- Each `accession` mixes fibroblasts and cardiomyocytes. A per-accession z-score would therefore be dominated by cell type,
  so consider giving each cell line its own accession before harmonising.
- `cohort_role` is `unassigned`. Decide its development or lockbox role before adding it to `public_keloid_cohorts.json`.
